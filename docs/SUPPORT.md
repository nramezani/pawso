# Pawso Support

For help with Pawso, email **pawso.support@gmail.com**. Tell us your phone
type, app version, what you were trying to do, and the error you saw. Please
do not email passwords, invitation or clinic links, complete veterinary
records, payment information, or government ID. If a screenshot helps, hide
private details first.

For account or privacy requests, email the same address with the subject
“Pawso privacy request.” We will ask only for the information needed to
verify that the request is yours.

In the app, use **Account** to export structured data or initiate account
deletion. Download any original veterinary files you need from **Medical
Records** separately before deleting the account.

Pawso is not a veterinary or emergency service. If your pet needs urgent
care, contact a veterinarian or emergency animal hospital now.
