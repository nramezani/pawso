# Pawso Privacy Policy

**Effective:** November 10, 2026

**Operator and privacy contact:** Nafiseh Ramezani, individual developer,
British Columbia, Canada — **pawso.support@gmail.com**

Pawso helps adults organize pet care and veterinary records. This policy
explains what information Pawso handles, why, who receives it, and how to
request access or deletion. Email the privacy contact above with questions or
complaints. We will verify account-related requests using only the information
reasonably needed to protect the account.

## Information Pawso handles

- **Account and household:** email, account ID, sign-in events, optional name,
  household membership and role, invitation delivery and care-action history.
  An anonymous account has an identifier even before an email is added.
- **Pet and care:** profile and optional photo, clinic and emergency contacts,
  medications, schedules, reminders, dose and task logs, allergies, symptoms,
  weight, notes and health timeline.
- **Documents and AI requests:** PDFs/images and text the owner uploads or a
  holder of an owner-created clinic link sends; extracted suggestions,
  owner-reviewed facts, optional questions and generated responses. Uploaded
  files may incidentally contain human contact or other personal details.
- **Technical information:** limited diagnostics (error fingerprint, platform,
  app version), security/rate-limit data and service logs. Hosting providers
  may process network metadata such as IP addresses to operate their services.

Do not upload unnecessary human medical, payment or government-ID information.
Only share someone else's information when you have authority to do so.

## Uses and sharing

We use the information to provide and secure the app, synchronize records,
coordinate household care, deliver invitations, schedule local reminders,
respond to support requests, prevent abuse and diagnose failures. Pawso does
not sell personal information or use private records for advertising profiles.
There are no paid subscriptions in the current app.

Records are visible to household members according to their role. An owner
can invite, remove or change members. An owner-created, expiring clinic link
lets its holder send one file for owner review; it does **not** verify the
sender's identity or grant access to existing pet records. Owners can revoke
unused links. When an owner intentionally exports or shares a file, the
recipient receives what the owner selected.

Service providers process data to operate Pawso: Supabase (accounts,
database, private file storage), Render (API hosting and logs), OpenAI
(optional user-requested AI processing), Resend (invitation email), Expo/EAS
(build and delivery services), and Apple or Google (app distribution). Pawso
limits access to these services and remains responsible for the information
they process on its behalf. Processing may occur outside Canada. We may also
disclose information when
required by law or to address a security or safety incident.

AI is optional. Before sending selected records or pet context for AI
extraction or assistance, the app asks for consent; users can reset that
consent in Account. AI output can be wrong and must be checked against the
original and with a veterinarian for medical decisions. Manual record keeping
remains available.

## Storage, retention and deletion

Active account and pet records remain while the account is in use. Archiving
a pet or document hides it from active views but retains it until permanent
deletion. The app offers document and pet deletion, structured-data export,
and account deletion in Account. Original veterinary files must be downloaded
separately. A successful permanent deletion removes the relevant primary
database records and stored files; an account with other household members
may need its owner to resolve their access before deletion can proceed.

Limited service and security logs are kept according to the applicable
provider configuration and then expire. Information may be retained longer
when a specific legal obligation or security investigation requires it. The
current production Supabase Free plan does not provide automatic daily
database backups. If backups or a different retention configuration are
introduced, we will update this policy before relying on a new deletion
timeline. Keep your own copies of important veterinary records: Pawso does
not guarantee recovery after an outage or accidental deletion.

An optional read-only offline snapshot can be enabled in Account. It is
stored in the app's device storage and removed when disabled, on sign-out or
account deletion. It is not an independently encrypted medical vault. Protect
your device with a passcode.

## Your choices and security

You may edit records, manage household access, revoke unused clinic links,
withdraw optional AI consent, export structured data, download originals and
initiate deletion in the app. Email **pawso.support@gmail.com** to request
access, correction, deletion, or help when you cannot sign in. We will explain
any legal or technical limits that apply to a particular request.

Pawso uses authentication, role-based database access, private file storage,
restricted file links, encrypted transport and API request limits. No online
service is risk-free. Protect your password, device and invitation or clinic
links, and report suspected unauthorized access to the contact above.

Pawso is intended for adults. It is not designed for children to create their
own accounts. We will update the effective date and communicate material
changes as required. Questions and privacy complaints can be sent to
**pawso.support@gmail.com**.
