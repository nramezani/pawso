## 1. Acceptance and eligibility

By creating an account or using Pawso, the user agrees to these Terms and the
Privacy Policy. Users must be legally able to enter a contract and must provide
accurate account information.

## 2. Pawso is an organizational tool, not veterinary care

Pawso organizes user-provided pet information and can generate AI-assisted
summaries and suggestions. Pawso does not diagnose, prescribe, establish a
veterinarian-client-patient relationship, monitor emergencies, or replace
professional veterinary judgment.

For urgent symptoms or possible poisoning, users must contact a veterinarian,
emergency veterinary hospital, or appropriate poison-control service
immediately. Users must not delay care because of Pawso output or reminders.

## 3. User responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

- Reviewing records, extracted fields, reminders, and AI output for accuracy.
- Confirming medication instructions directly with the prescribing veterinarian.
- Keeping account credentials and household invitation codes secure.
- Having authority to upload and share information.
- Maintaining backups or official copies of important veterinary records.
- Using household roles only for people who genuinely need access.

## 4. Acceptable use

Users may not use Pawso to break the law, infringe rights, access another
household without permission, upload malware, probe security, evade limits,
resell unauthorized access, submit harmful or deceptive content, or use AI
features to obtain or distribute veterinary prescriptions or unsafe treatment
instructions.

## 5. Accounts and household access

The account holder is responsible for activity under their account. Owners
control household invitations and roles. Caregivers and sitters receive limited
access selected by the product's permissions. Pawso may suspend compromised,
abusive, or unlawful accounts.

## 6. User content and permission to operate the service

Users retain ownership of content they submit. They grant Pawso a limited,
non-exclusive permission to host, process, transform, transmit, and display that
content only as needed to provide, secure, support, and improve the service as
described in the Privacy Policy. Pawso must not claim ownership of veterinary
records or use private records for advertising.

## 7. AI features

AI features may produce inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or inappropriate
content. Sources shown in Pawso indicate which supplied records informed an
answer; they do not prove medical correctness. Users must review outputs and
seek professional veterinary advice.

## 8. Subscriptions and billing

Paid plans, prices, included usage, billing frequency, renewal terms, taxes,
trial terms, and cancellation methods will be shown before purchase. In-app
subscriptions will be processed by the applicable app store unless otherwise
stated. Cancellation stops future renewal but ordinarily does not create a
refund for the current billing period, except where required by law or app-store
policy. Core access to export or delete user data must not depend on a paid plan.

## 9. Service availability and changes

Pawso may add, change, limit, or discontinue features. Reasonable notice should
be provided for material changes that reduce paid functionality. Pawso does not
guarantee uninterrupted operation, reminder delivery, data extraction, or AI
availability.

applicable intellectual-property laws. These Terms do not transfer Pawso
intellectual property to users.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Pawso is provided on an “as available”
basis without warranties that it will be error-free or medically accurate.
Liability language, exclusions, consumer-law savings clauses, and any monetary

Users may stop using Pawso and request deletion. Pawso may suspend or terminate
