## What Pawso provides

Pawso organizes information that users enter or upload about their pets,
including care tasks, medications, reminders, medical records and household
handoffs. Optional AI features can summarize selected information. Pawso is
**not** a veterinary clinic or emergency monitoring service. It does not
diagnose, prescribe, verify a clinic's identity or replace a veterinarian.
If your pet may need urgent care, contact a veterinarian or emergency animal
hospital immediately; do not wait for an app alert or AI answer.

## Accounts and responsibilities

or that a file is accurate. The owner must review it.
