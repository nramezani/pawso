# Pawso Membership and Pricing Recommendation

**Status:** Proposed—not implemented  
**Currency:** CAD for the initial Canadian launch  
**Decision date:** September 21, 2026

## Recommendation

Run the private beta free. Do not charge until account deletion, privacy
controls, native notifications, monitoring, and the complete recovery flow pass
physical-device testing.

At launch, use a simple three-level model:

| Plan | Proposed price | Best for | Included value |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Pawso Free | $0 | Trying Pawso | 2 pets, manual profiles/timeline/care, basic reminders, 2 document extractions/month, 5 Ask requests/month |
| Pawso Plus | $5.99/month or $49.99/year | Individual pet households | Up to 6 pets, 15 extractions/month, 50 Ask requests/month, Smart Care, trends, vet reports, refill reminders |
| Pawso Family | $9.99/month or $84.99/year | Multi-person care | Plus features, up to 10 pets, 6 household members, caregiver handoffs, household activity history, 30 extractions/month, 100 Ask requests/month |

Use localized store prices outside Canada. Do not advertise AI as unlimited;
cost controls and fair-use limits should be clear.

## What must always remain free

- Viewing and exporting a user's own records.
- Correcting or deleting data and deleting the account.
- Safety warnings and access to confirmed medication instructions.
- Access needed to avoid locking a household out of its existing records.
- A reasonable manual pet-care experience.

## Trial and launch approach

1. **Private beta:** All features free; measure activation, weekly retention,
   extraction cost, Ask cost, and household adoption.
2. **Public beta:** Keep beta users on a time-limited Founding plan; test plan
   descriptions without charging first.
3. **Paid launch:** Offer a 14-day Plus trial without hiding cancellation.
4. **Annual emphasis:** Make annual pricing the best value but keep monthly available.
5. **No ads in medical context:** Do not place targeted ads beside pet health,
   medication, symptom, or emergency information.

## Membership rules

- Entitlements belong to the household, not each caregiver.
- The household owner manages billing.
- Losing paid status must not delete data; accounts become read-only only for
  over-limit premium creation, with export and deletion preserved.
- Define what happens when a household exceeds downgraded pet/member limits.
- Usage counters must be visible before the limit is reached.
- Failed AI requests should not consume a quota.
- Store purchases require server-side receipt validation before beta charging.

## Why this structure

Pawso's differentiated value is the combination of records, grounded AI,
daily care, and household coordination. Charging separately for every small
feature would make the product feel stressful and transactional. The proposed
prices stay below hardware-connected pet subscriptions while leaving room for
AI and storage costs.

Public benchmarks change and must be rechecked before launch. Current examples
include Whisker Plus at US$7.99/month or US$79.99/year and device-based services
such as Fi starting around US$14/month; Pawso has no included hardware and
should launch below those levels.

## Metrics required before final pricing

- Seven-day and 30-day retention.
- Percentage of households adding a second pet or member.
- Monthly AI and storage cost per active household.
- Document extraction success and correction rates.
- Number of active care days per month.
- Conversion intent at $4.99, $5.99, and $7.99 CAD.
- Support cost, refund rate, and churn reasons.

## Do not build yet

Avoid marketplace commissions, advertising, tele-vet sales, or sponsored
recommendations during the first paid version. They complicate trust, privacy,
moderation, and veterinary neutrality before the core product is proven.
